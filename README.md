# Namma Kannada

**Speak. Understand. Learn.**

Namma Kannada helps visitors, students, and newcomers explore everyday Kannada through practical phrases, pronunciation support, learning activities, and Karnataka cultural information.

## What to Review

- **Home:** Quick access to the app's main learning and travel features.
- **Situations:** Useful Kannada phrases for everyday scenarios such as transport, food, shopping, and directions.
- **Learn:** Kannada alphabet, numbers, and learning activities such as flashcards or quizzes where available.
- **Speak and Listen:** Phrase pronunciation and voice-related features, depending on device support.
- **Travel Tools:** Auto fare simulator, signboard assistant, and emergency phrase kit where available.
- **Culture and Festivals:** Information about Karnataka's traditions, greetings, and festivals.
- **Profile:** User preferences and app settings.

## 2. Enable Kannada Voice Support (MUST)

If Kannada audio is unavailable or silent, follow these steps on your Android phone:

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Navigate to **Accessibility** or **System / General Management → Text-to-speech output**. You can also search Settings for “Text-to-speech”.
3. Tap the settings icon next to the preferred engine, such as **Speech Services by Google**.
4. Select **Install voice data**.
5. Find **Kannada (India)** and download it if available.
6. Return to the app, turn up **Media Volume**, and test audio playback. Ensure the phone is not muted.

Menu names and Kannada voice availability may vary by device. The app uses the speech capabilities available on the device; downloading voice data may be necessary.
